Patrol route

Choose a task type, click along the route, then select Finish & save patrol tasks.

Camera sectors show theoretical coverage only; obstacles, terrain and sensor performance are not modelled.

Objects and layers

Toggle visibility, select an object to edit it, or click × to delete. Undo restores deleted objects.

Asset catalogue

Select a preset when placing an asset. Import/export CSV to maintain equipment specifications. Camera detection footprints require calibration.

CSV columns: name,type,range_m,fov_deg,colour,notes. Values are editable in Excel or a text editor.

Map calibration

1. Open Google Maps and switch to satellite view.
2. Find a recognisable ground feature. Right-click it, then click the latitude and longitude at the top of the menu to copy both coordinates.
3. Select Calibrate and click the same feature on our survey map.
4. Paste the coordinates below and click Add reference point.
5. Repeat for a second point as far away as practical.

Two-point alignment is approximate; use surveyed coordinates or georeferenced imagery for precision.

Map not calibrated

You can still place guards, assets and draw routes and zones. Camera detection coverage, real-world distances and estimated patrol durations are unavailable until the map is calibrated.
